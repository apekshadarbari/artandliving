Policies

Privacy

We collect your name and email when you sign up for our newsletter, book a workshop, or send a message through the contact form. This information is never sold. Email is managed through Flodesk, workshop bookings and payments are handled through Stan Store, and the contact form is processed through Netlify. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time by clicking the link in any email.

Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. It's cookieless and doesn't collect any personal or identifying information. We also use Google Analytics 4, which uses cookies to track pageviews and interactions like clicking a button or an outside link (for example, "Reserve Your Spot" or "Join the Circle"), so we can understand what's working and what isn't.

Advertising and retargeting. We use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel to understand site activity and to show relevant ads to past visitors on Facebook and Instagram. If you fill out a form on this site, information like your name, email, or phone number may be securely hashed and shared with Meta to help match you to your Facebook or Instagram account for this purpose — sensitive information (financial, health, or government ID data) is never included. You can control how Meta uses this information through your Facebook ad preferences.

You're welcome to reach out any time if you have questions about your information or want it removed — email me directly and I'll take care of it.
