1:1 Therapeutic Art Session — CA$95

Come, sit with me — one to one, 75 minutes.

THE EXPERIENCE This is the room, made private. Just you and me, online.

You may not want a group. You might just want my complete attention and a safe, quiet space to come into yourself. We shape this session around what you personally want and need. That is what this is.

THE HOST After years of burning out in tech and a late ADHD diagnosis, I found creating art as my compass back home. Now I work as an artist and certified Therapeutic Arts Practitioner, building the room I needed when I was running on fumes.

WHAT HAPPENS IN THE SESSION We start by settling in, a few minutes to land and leave the day at the door. Then, after checking in, an art prompt to orient you, and from there you follow what comes. I can make alongside you, or simply hold space while you do. Near the end we slow down together, and if you want to, we talk about what came up. Everything here is an invitation, never an expectation. This is your time.

WHAT YOU'LL NEED You'll get a basic supplies list after we do the intake, but nothing fancy to start. Usually we can work with what you already have at home. If we learn there's something specific worth exploring, I'll guide you on what might suit it better as we go.

The Practical Bits

  • 75 minutes, one to one, online, via Zoom — link sent after booking
  • Pick a time that works when you book. If none of the slots fit, just reach out and we'll find something that does
  • A short intake after you book, so I can meet you where you are
  • Cancellations and reschedules need at least 48 hours' notice
  • No skills needed. No wrong way to do this. Come as you are.

I'll see you in the room.
