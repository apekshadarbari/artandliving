A Therapeutic Arts Preview Workshop: Founding Circle

A Room Safe to Let It Go

For the woman who is tired of holding it all together.

The Room — Studio @ The Garden Strathcona

The Experience: Therapeutic Arts

An intimate, guided experience for reconnecting with yourself and your body through creating art in community with other women. Two hours, just for you, to express something honest rather than make something pretty.

The Host: Apeksha Darbari

After years of burning out in tech and a late ADHD diagnosis, I found creating art as my compass back home. Now, I work as an artist and a certified Therapeutic Arts Practitioner, building the room I needed when I was running on fumes.

What Happens in the Room

You'll make something with your hands in the company of other women who are also, finally, putting something down they've been carrying too long. I'll be there as your guide and companion, holding the space while you explore what your body wants to say and put it on the page.

Nothing is required except your presence. No skills needed. Nothing to get right.

All supplies included — watercolours, markers, oil pastels, charcoal, crayons. You get to work with whatever calls to you.

Tell Me More

Is This Right for Me?

This is for any woman who is looking to bring more connection and creativity into her life. A lot of us are living at different stages of burnout with varying stress levels and thirteen thousand reasons for them. And often with no time or space to recharge, to do what we want to do, to prioritize our wants and needs over everyone else's expectations.

Even more of us are missing a community - our tribe of women, the essential female friendships we were never taught to prioritize, the village we forgot we had, and need.

This is my humble attempt to create the space and build that village, starting with this room. You belong here if this speaks to you.

What This Isn't

This is neither therapy nor a replacement for it. I see therapy as important, hard work. It is examining, making sense of, and changing long standing behaviour and belief systems.

Here, we play. We create a safe, supportive space to allow that tiny voice to speak without trying to correct it. There's no way to fail at this. We follow our body and spirit and let the mind rest or wander, without performance, without the goal to accomplish anything, with the single intention to be as you are now, not become something better. This present version of you deserves to exist as she is too.

As a Therapeutic Arts Practitioner, I trained to facilitate these sessions ethically and in an informed way, but my biggest qualification as this space holder is having walked this path myself. I'm still on it, and would be honoured to be your companion and guide, as you get started.

What This Room Holds

This is a guided experience where I hold the space for you so you don't have to hold anything for some time. You're seen, you're heard, you're paid attention to. You deserve some time where you're being cared for too.

I built this room like the rooms I want to be in — with no pressure to perform, no expectation to mask, no box to fit in; a room where I can be whimsical, funny, tired, angry, sad, and I don't have to pretend to be anything else; a room where I don't have to negotiate how much of me is allowed to be there; a room I don't have to contort myself to fit in. I built this room for the wonderful, unique, beautiful you, in all your glory. I built this room for us.
