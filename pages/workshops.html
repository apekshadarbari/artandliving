A Room Safe to Let It Go

For the woman who is tired of constantly holding it all together.

The Room — Studio @ The Garden Strathcona

The Experience: Therapeutic Arts

An intimate, guided experience for reconnecting with yourself and your body through creating art in community with other women. Two hours, just for you, to express something honest rather than make something pretty.

The Host: Apeksha Darbari

After years of burning out in tech and a late ADHD diagnosis, I found creating art as my compass back home. Now, I work as an artist and a certified Therapeutic Arts Practitioner, building the room I needed when I was running on fumes.

Tell Me More

What Happens in the Room

You'll make something with your hands in the company of other women who are also, finally, putting something down they've been carrying too long. I'll be there as a guide and companion, holding the space while you explore what your body wants to say and put it on the page.

Nothing is required except your presence — no skills needed, nothing to get right. Watercolours, markers, oil pastels, charcoal, crayons: work with whatever calls to you, all supplies included.

Short Meditation – Check in – The Making – Sharing Circle – Reflections & Soft Close

Everything here is an invitation, never an expectation.

What This Isn't

This is a non-clinical therapeutic arts session — not a replacement for therapy, but rather a wonderful companion to it. I'm not a licensed therapist; my training is as a space holder and facilitator, but my biggest qualification is having walked this path myself.

Here, we play. We let that tiny voice speak before we try to correct it, and follow the body and spirit without a goal to accomplish. This version of you, exactly as she is, doesn't need fixing. I'd be honoured to guide you.
