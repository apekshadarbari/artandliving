What to expect

A session that feels
like permission

Every part of this is paced on purpose. Nothing is rushed, and the slack in the schedule is not an accident — it's the design. Your nervous system can't soften on a clock.


No art experience needed. The space is small and intentional — never more than 7–8 women in a group, or you're welcome to book a private 1:1 session if you're not ready for a group. You will be met where you are. You will be witnessed and supported, not instructed or evaluated.

  • 01

    Arrival + stretching · 8–10 min

    We start by landing in the body. A little movement, a little stretch, no pressure to arrive as anything in particular. You don't have to be ready. You just have to be here.

  • 02

    Meditation · under 10 min, unhurried

    A short settling. Long enough to drop below the noise, never so long it becomes another thing to get right.

  • 03

    The reframe · 5–6 min

    A gentle turn in how we're holding the making — permission over performance, sensation over outcome. This is where "I'm not creative" gets quietly put down.

  • 04

    The making · 60–65 min, protected

    The heart of it. Following instinct, colours, and your body, not chasing a specific result. The moment where creativity flows in safety.

  • 05

    Sharing + soft close · 10–12 min

    A circle to land what moved, if you want to speak — and full permission to simply witness if you don't. We close soft, so you leave regulated, not raw.
