What to expect

A session that feels
like permission

No art experience needed. No right way to show up. Whatever materials you have at home are the right materials. You make alongside me, camera on or off, sharing only what you want to share.


The space is small and intentional — never more than 7–8 women online. You will be witnessed. You will not be fixed.

  • 01

    Landing meditation

    A short guided moment to leave the day behind and arrive in your body.

  • 02

    The warm-up

    A gentle reframe to ease into making mode. A little of what the science says about creativity and the nervous system.

  • 03

    The making

    One gentle prompt. Whatever materials you have. Apeksha makes alongside you. No watching, no evaluating. Just presence.

  • 04

    Sharing circle

    Always an invitation, never an expectation. You share only if you feel called to. Your presence is all that is needed.

  • 05

    Soft close

    Not what did you make but what did you notice. A quiet moment to transition back out and leave when you're ready.
