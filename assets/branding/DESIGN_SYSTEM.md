## Typography

### Display: Playfair Display
- Use for: titles, headings, pull quotes, permission statements, italic accents
- Weights in use: 400 (regular), 500 (medium)
- Italic: yes — use freely for poetry, quotes, card headers
- Fallback: Georgia, serif

### Body: Nunito Sans
- Use for: body copy, captions, labels, CTAs, navigation, metadata
- Weights in use: 300 (light), 400 (regular), 600 (semibold)
- Fallback: Proxima Nova, Open Sans, sans-serif

### Type Scale (PDF / A5 context)

| Role                  | Font             | Size      | Weight | Style  | Tracking    |
|-----------------------|------------------|-----------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Cover title           | Playfair Display | 42–52pt   | 400    | Normal | –           |
| Permission statement  | Playfair Display | 28–36pt   | 400    | Normal | –           |
| Section heading       | Playfair Display | 22–28pt   | 400    | Normal | –           |
| Pull quote / poem     | Playfair Display | 16–18pt   | 400    | Italic | –           |
| Body copy             | Nunito Sans      | 10–11pt   | 300    | Normal | –           |
| Caption / label       | Nunito Sans      | 8–9pt     | 400    | Normal | 0.15em      |
| Eyebrow / tag         | Nunito Sans      | 7–8pt     | 400    | Normal | 0.2em caps  |

## Design Principles

- **Warmth over sterility** — always lean into the warm palette (Slate, Clay) over cool neutrals
- **Serif for soul** — Playfair Display carries the emotional weight; use it for anything that needs to land
- **White space is nourishment** — generous margins, don't crowd the page
- **Clay for action** — CTAs, accents, and eye-catchers always in Clay
- **Midnight for depth** — dark backgrounds are used intentionally for immersive, contained moments
- **British/Canadian spelling** — honour, colour, nourishment (never wellness)
