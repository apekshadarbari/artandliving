The Artist

Creating spaces for the
woman I used to be

The cost of building a life we did not intentionally choose is steeper than we let ourselves believe. A life lacking the space to breathe, the bandwidth to process, or the safety to be. The life that led me to burning out and a late neurodivergent diagnosis that made me see why. That is when I decided, I am not going to start a new chapter. I will rewrite the whole book.

My life is to honour my nature,
To relish the fruit from my tree,
For that is all I have to offer,
To always be more of me.

I'm Apeksha — an abstract artist, a certified Therapeutic Arts Practitioner, and many other things but primarily, the woman building spaces where women stop performing and start being. A safe space for the woman who is the safe space for everyone else but has no one who is hers. I'm building a community where she can tap into her body and out of her mind into the present moment by creating art around others like her. I'm creating what I needed (and still do).
